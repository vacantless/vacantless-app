S746b: referral credit, $99 to both sides on the first paid month

The referral loop (S355) recorded who referred whom and paid nothing for
it. Engine 1 of the subscriber plan (approved 2026-11-03) needs every
landlord to have a reason to send the link.

- When a referred landlord pays a first invoice above $0, the referrer
  and the referred account each get a CA$99 Stripe customer-balance
  credit (one month of Growth). A balance credit stacks with any coupon
  and comes off the next bill, so no second coupon or trial rule.
- Migration 0267: org_invites.referral_credit_at and
  referral_credit_cents make the grant idempotent (claim the row before
  paying; a referee paid without the referrer is noted, never repaid).
- Webhook: invoice.paid runs the grant after applySubscription; never
  throws into the event.
- Refer a landlord page: the offer in one line, "Credit applied" on the
  row once both sides are paid. The page is still behind
  REFERRALS_ENABLED=1 (Vercel), turned on separately.

Tests: scripts/test-referral-credit.ts (23 checks).
