S748b: server-rendered date labels name a time zone

Pages run on Vercel in UTC. 23 date labels (documents uploaded and
expiring, settings "last checked", referrals, team invites, Rotessa and
Stripe sync times, the repair log, admin lists) were formatted with no
time zone, so anything after 7 PM Eastern showed as the next day.

Audit of every "America/Toronto" in the code (the S742 review counted 71
files): almost all are the fallback after the account's own zone
(organizations.booking_timezone, set from the first rental by 0268), and
reminders, bookings, viewing times, send hours and follow-ups already use
it. The Ontario legal forms (N1, N4, Standard Lease) stay on Toronto on
purpose.

- lib/dates.ts: DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE and displayTimeZone().
- The 23 labels pass timeZone: DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE.
- scripts/test-s748-dates.ts fails on any new server-side date format
  without a time zone (six known-safe plain-date labels allowed by line).
