S748a: background failures stop looking like successes; page crashes are recorded

From the S742 review, engineering item 2: a sweep that hit a database
error answered 200 { ok: false }, sweep-all counted it as a success, and
the morning health email said all clear. 33 of 36 cron routes declared no
time limit, and page crashes lived only in Vercel's logs.

- lib/cron-sweeps.ts: sweepFailed() counts 200 + ok:false as a failure
  (label "path:not_ok"); summaries list sweeps that took 40 s or more;
  details keep each sweep's time and error text; repeatFailures() finds
  a sweep that failed in two runs in a row.
- sweep-all reads each sweep's ok and error, logs slow sweeps, and emails
  the admins at once when a sweep fails twice in a row (once per sweep
  per Toronto day, claimed in platform_digest_runs).
- Every cron route now exports maxDuration = 60.
- Page crashes: app/error.tsx and app/global-error.tsx show a plain
  "Something went wrong" page with Try again, and post the crash to
  /api/report-error (capped per connection, query strings dropped) which
  writes app_errors (migration 0269, service role only). The health email
  gets a "Page crashes" section and prunes the log at 30 days.

Checked before the change: no sweep answered ok:false in the last 24
hours, so this adds no new alarms on day one.

Tests: scripts/test-s748-honest-sweeps.ts (40 checks); test-health-digest
and test-sweep-timer unchanged and passing.
