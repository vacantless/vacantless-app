S748c: the Stripe webhook takes each event once

From the S742 review, engineering item 9: the webhook verified signatures
but had no event-id check and relied on every write being idempotent.
Stripe delivers at least once, retries on any error or timeout, and the
platform and Connect destinations can both deliver.

- lib/stripe-event-claim.ts: claimStripeEvent inserts the event id into
  stripe_webhook_events (migration 0270, applied). new = process,
  duplicate = acknowledge with 200 and skip, unknown (table unreachable)
  = process anyway. releaseStripeEvent deletes the claim when a run fails
  so Stripe's retry is processed. Claims older than 60 days are pruned.
- The route claims right after the signature check, before the rent and
  billing paths, and releases on a 500.

Tests: scripts/test-s748-stripe-event-claim.ts (16); test-stripe-webhook
stubs the new module.
