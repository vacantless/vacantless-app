S815a: a rental site's email with a quoted sender check is passed on

Found live 2026-11-17: Rentals.ca's sign-up email reached a landlord's
Vacantless address and was not passed on to the owner (S806 pass-on). The
mail server wrote smtp.mailfrom in quotes; the closing quote stayed on the
domain ("em3909.rentals.ca\""), so the email read as unaligned and was
dropped as "not parsed".

- lib/portal-senders.ts: domainOf strips quotes and angle brackets before
  reading the domain. A look-alike domain is still refused.
- scripts/test-site-mail-pass.ts: the real header shape, and a look-alike.
