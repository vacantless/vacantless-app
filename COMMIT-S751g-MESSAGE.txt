S751g: Forms N6 (illegal act or false income) and L10 (former tenant owes), official 2026/09

- Tenancy page: "Form N6". Reasons (any combination): drugs, another
  illegal act or business, false income in rent-geared-to-income housing.
  End date at least 10 days for drugs only, otherwise 20 (14 for a second
  notice after an N5 that gave 7 days to fix, on record or confirmed);
  delivery days added. Up to 3 events, each checked against its box on the
  form at 9 pt. Prepare / record service / void like the N5; frozen in
  notices.snapshot (type N6, already allowed).
- L2: a served N6 can be filed once it counts as given; Part 2 ticks N6.

L10 (S751h folded in):
- Tenancy page: "Form L10" for a tenant who moved out (move-out date on or
  after 2021-09-01, filed within a year of it). Rent owing comes from the
  ledger counted to the move-out date (same table rules as the N4 / L1 /
  L9), plus optional damage and serious-interference costs, each with its
  explanation (checked against the 572x104 box at 9 pt). The former
  tenant's new address goes on the form when known; the rental unit is
  refused as that address. Fee $186 portal / $201 paper; $50,000 cap.
- NSF and utilities need their own tables and bills, so those lines stay
  blank for the landlord.
- POST /dashboard/tenancies/[id]/l10/official returns the filled L10;
  nothing is stored or filed.
