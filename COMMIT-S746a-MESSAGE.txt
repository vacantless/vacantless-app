S746a: free Ontario rent-increase calculator with the Form N1 filled in

Engine 1 of the subscriber plan (approved 2026-11-03). Every Ontario
landlord with a tenant serves a rent-increase notice once a year and
searches for the guideline and Form N1 the month they do it. Until now
that engine sat behind sign-in. This puts it on www.vacantless.com as a
free tool, no account, with "Start free" as the only capture.

- /tools/rent-increase: rent and move-in date in, the guideline for the
  effective year, the new rent, the first legal day and the notice
  deadline out. A wanted start day is honoured when the 12-month and
  90-day rules allow it, otherwise the legal day is used and said so.
  Exempt (post 2018-11-15) units take the landlord's chosen rent.
- /tools/rent-increase/n1 and /n1/official: a printable review copy and
  the official LTB Form N1 (v.01/04/2022) filled from the same inputs,
  recomputed on the server from the query so the form always matches
  the engine. Nothing is stored. noindex.
- lib/rent-increase-tool.ts: pure parse, compute, snapshot and sentence
  (inputs cleaned and capped before any PDF).
- Footer link, sitemap entry (priority 0.9), ad tags fire on /tools/*
  so the pages can be the Google Ads landing pages.
- Guideline table on the page reads rent_guidelines over the shipped
  constant; 2027 = 1.9% verified against ontario.ca 2026-11-03.

Test: scripts/test-rent-increase-tool.ts (59 checks).
