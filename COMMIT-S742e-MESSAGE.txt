S742e: when a rental rents, the renters still waiting are told

Bruce 303 leased on 2026-10-31 and Laine, Mandy and Samantha were still
"New" on it the next day. Nothing wrote to a waiting renter when a
rental was marked leased: the public page switched to "no longer
available" and that was all.

Now both lease-up paths (the rental's status save and a new tenancy)
call notifyWaitingRentersRented: every renter still at New, Replied or
Contacted on that rental, who asked within 60 days and left an email,
gets one branded email saying it has been rented, with up to three of
the account's other open homes and a "See it and book a viewing" button
on each (or an honest "nothing else is open today, reply and we will
keep you in mind"). One email per address, at most 25 per rental. Every
open request on the rented unit then closes as Lost, emailed or not, and
the timeline gets one "Rented notice sent" line per email. The new kind
is marketing, so an unsubscribed renter is skipped. Best effort: an
email failure never fails the lease-up.
