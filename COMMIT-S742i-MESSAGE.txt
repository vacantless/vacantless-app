S742i: when nobody can book, Today opens times in one tap

Agile ran ten days with four live rentals, 116 waiting renters and no
viewing time open. Today said the right thing since S740 ("Renters
can't book a viewing") and then offered only a link to a form. The
second move was missing.

Under that row the Today card now offers three one-tap presets (Weekday
evenings 5 to 7 pm, Weekdays in the day 10 am to 4 pm, Saturday
mornings 10 am to 1 pm), "Pick my own times", and, when the account has
showing agents, "Have a showing agent cover them". A preset inserts the
weekly windows it does not already have (an overlapping window on that
weekday is left alone), stamps availability_reopened_at, and returns to
Today with a green line that says what opened and that renters waiting
on a rental in a building that shows on those days get the new times
by email within 15 minutes (the existing availability-reopened sweep).
