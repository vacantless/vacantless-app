S743b: Ontario Standard Lease (2229E) filled from the tenancy

Ontario requires its Standard Form of Lease for most residential tenancies.
The lease vault wrote the additional terms and signed them, but never
produced the form itself; liv.rent and others do.

Each lease document now has an "Ontario Standard Lease" page: the 2229E
questions the tenancy does not hold (notice address, email notices,
emergency contact, how rent is paid, services and utilities, key deposit,
smoking, insurance), saved on the lease (migration 0264,
lease_documents.standard_form) and carried from the landlord's last lease.
The download prints the tenancy and those answers onto the government's
flat 2229E (2020/12, unmodified) and attaches the clauses as Section 15.

The fillable 2229E is a dynamic XFA form no PDF library fills and the
static one is encrypted, so values are drawn onto the flat copy at the
static copy's field boxes (read with pdf.js). Pages 1 to 6 line up to the
pixel; page 7 rows were measured from the flat page. Checked by rendering a
filled sample of every page. Flags an NSF charge over $20 and a deposit over
one month's rent, the form's own limits.
