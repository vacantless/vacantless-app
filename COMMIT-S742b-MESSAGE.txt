S742b: the public renter form gets a limit and a hidden field

The form at /r/<id> is open to anyone, and each submit emails the
landlord, emails the renter, may call the AI once and may book and text.
Nothing capped it except texts (500 a month). One script could flood a
landlord's inbox, spend the email and AI budget, and use the landlord's
own sender as a relay.

Now: a hidden field people never see (scripts that fill every box get a
quiet non-answer); 12 submits per connection per 10 minutes; 40 submits
per rental per hour; 20 AI answers per rental per hour. All far above a
real renter's use. The counter is a service-role function (migration
0263, public_submit_hit). Every check fails OPEN: no function, no
service key or a database error lets the renter through, so the code is
safe to deploy before the migration runs. A blocked submit sees a plain
"too many requests, wait a few minutes" line.

Tests: scripts/test-public-submit-limit.ts (23).
