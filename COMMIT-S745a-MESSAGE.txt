S745a: only Vacantless can change an account's plan and billing

A signed-in landlord, or a teammate, could set their own plan to Premium
through the public database API: every member may update the account row
and the plan, subscription and Stripe columns had no guard. Feature
switches had the same gap (an override beats the plan).

App, deployed before migration 0266:
- startPilot and ensureCustomer write through the service role, after the
  owner-only check.
- The staff feature switch writes organization_feature_flags through the
  service role.

Migration 0266 (applied after this deploy): a trigger refuses signed-in
changes to plan, subscription, Stripe ids, pilot deposit, concierge,
closed and texting-number retry fields. One move stays open: the
create_organization default 'trial' down to 'free', which sign-up and
the agent quick onboard make. Landlords lose write access to
organization_feature_flags.

Test: scripts/test-billing-columns-locked.ts (18 checks).
