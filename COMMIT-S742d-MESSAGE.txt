S742d: closing an account removes its secrets the same minute

Close this account used to put rentals off market, stamp closed_at,
remove logins and email us, and leave behind the encrypted site logins
(Kijiji, Zumper, Rentals.ca and the Facebook Page token), the Plaid
access tokens, any open sign-in window, and the texting number (released
only when the daily sweep noticed). The /data-deletion page promised
more than that.

New lib/account-closure-server.ts wipeOrgSecrets: deletes the channel
sessions and sign-in windows, deletes the bank tokens and marks the
connections disconnected, pauses every posting account, and gives the
texting number back to Twilio (the sweep retries if Twilio is down).
Each step is independent; one failure is reported and the rest still
runs. The closure email to us now says what was removed. The landlord's
records (renters, tenancies, documents) stay for the 30-day deletion as
before.

Tests: scripts/test-account-closure-server.ts (15).
