S736: Marketplace kit on the phone (review item 9)

Most small landlords post from the Facebook app, so the kit now works on a
phone end to end.

- Photos: on a phone, step 2 saves every photo through the share sheet
  ("Save N images"). Where that is not offered, a grid to press and hold;
  a computer keeps the zip.
- Getting there: the rental page on a computer shows a code to scan and an
  "Email me the link" button. Both open /kit/<signed token>, a no-sign-in kit
  page that works for 14 days (lib/marketplace-phone-link, HMAC, nothing
  stored), kept out of search.
- Step 4: the facebook.com/share/... link the Facebook app copies is followed
  to the ad's own /marketplace/item/<id>/ link before the save check
  (resolveMarketplaceAdUrl), for the landlord, agent and phone saves.
- One facts builder and one rental loader for every kit page.

Tests: test-marketplace-phone (60), test-marketplace-kit, listing-distribution,
marketplace-helper; plain-language baseline records the two new surfaces.
