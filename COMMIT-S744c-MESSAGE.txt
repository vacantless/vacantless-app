S744c: Ontario Standard Lease signed on page 7, frozen at send

2229E slice 2. S743b printed the form but left Section 17 blank, and it
read live tenancy fields even after the lease went out for signature.

- Page 7: each SIGNED lease_signers row is stamped in its own row. The
  landlord row for the landlord; a tenant on the row printed with their
  name, else the first blank row. Never on a row printed with someone
  else's name: an unmatched signer (or a fifth tenant) is listed on the
  attachment instead. Typed signatures print as text; drawn ones as their
  PNG, fitted to the box. A broken image leaves the box for wet ink.
- Date column: the day signed, in Toronto, in the form's yyyy/mm/dd.
- Frozen at send: sendLeaseForSignature saves the Standard Form input in
  rendered_snapshot.standardForm. Once the lease is not a draft, the PDF
  prints from that copy and the snapshot's clauses. Withdraw already
  clears the snapshot, so a corrected draft reads live again. Older sent
  leases without the copy still print from live data.
