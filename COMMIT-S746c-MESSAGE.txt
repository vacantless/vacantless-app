S746c: free Ontario Standard Lease (2229E) filler

Engine 1 of the subscriber plan, tool 2. Every private residential
tenancy in Ontario signed since April 30, 2018 uses the government's
Standard Form of Lease, and landlords search for it on every new
tenancy. The signed-in product already fills the real form; this puts
the same fill on www.vacantless.com with no sign-in and nothing stored.

- /tools/standard-lease: one plain form (no JavaScript) with the same
  field names as the signed-in details form, plus the tenancy basics
  and Section 15 additional terms. POST to ./pdf returns the official
  2229E filled; the form's own limits (NSF over $20, deposit over one
  month, fixed term without an end) refuse with a plain page that says
  what to fix. Blank answers print blank.
- lib/standard-lease-tool.ts: pure read of the form, cleaned and
  capped, reusing detailsFromForm, standardLeaseMissing and
  standardLeaseProblems.
- Footer link and sitemap entry; the two tools link each other.

Test: scripts/test-standard-lease-tool.ts (36 checks).
