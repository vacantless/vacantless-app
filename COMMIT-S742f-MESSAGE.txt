S742f: N4 pay-by date follows Bill 60 (7 days since 2026-09-21)

lib/n4.ts still gave a monthly tenancy 14 days between service and the
N4 termination date. Bill 60 made it 7 days for every tenancy from
September 21, 2026 [verified 2026-11-01 via two news reports; confirm
against the LTB's current N4 instructions before serving]. Over-notice
does not void an N4, so nothing prepared so far is wrong, but the
"earliest lawful date" the product showed was a week late.

minN4NoticeDays now takes the service date: 14 (monthly and longer) or
7 (daily, weekly) before 2026-09-21, and 7 for every tenancy from that
day. deriveN4TerminationDate passes the date through. The N4 section
says so in plain words and tells the landlord the form on file is the
2022 N4, to check tribunalsontario.ca for the current one before
serving (the LTB replaced several forms in 2026).
