S748d: rentals, viewings and reconcile stay right past 1,000 rows

From the S742 review, engineering item 5. The database answers at most
1,000 rows per request, so pages that read a whole table were not only
slow for a 300-unit account, they were silently wrong:
- Rentals counted inquiries and photos by downloading every lead and
  photo row (300 units x 10 photos = 3,000 rows), so counts and the
  photos-ready check fell short.
- Viewings read every viewing oldest first, so past 1,000 the NEWEST
  (upcoming) viewings were the ones dropped.
- Reconcile read every bank transaction oldest first: same problem.

- Migration 0271 (applied): property_rollups(p_org), a grouped count run
  as the signed-in user (row security still applies), plus indexes for
  the reads below. Checked on Agile as a member: 286 inquiries and 130
  photos, identical to the old count.
- Rentals list uses the rollup.
- Viewings: everything from 8 days ago on, in full (upcoming plus the
  agent-load week), and the newest 200 older viewings for Past.
- Reconcile: the newest 1,000 transactions (shown oldest first as
  before), the expenses and rent payments linked to them (150 ids per
  read), and unlinked expenses dated from 15 days before the oldest one.

Work orders already read newest first, so the cap only drops very old
ones; the remaining small tables (rentals, tenancies, rules) stay well
under it.
